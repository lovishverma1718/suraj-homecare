About Suraj Home Care

Care with dignity, warmth and respect — right at home.

At Suraj Home Care Ltd., we believe every person deserves to age gracefully in the place they love most. Like the sun that rises every morning, our caregivers bring warmth, reliability and a bright presence into each home we serve.

From a few hours of companionship to round-the-clock support, we create care plans that respect your loved one's independence, culture and routine.

  • Carefully screened, trained & compassionate caregivers
  • Care plans tailored to medical, personal & cultural needs
  • Regular updates and open communication with families

Who We Are

A home care company built on heart.

Suraj means "sun" — and that's exactly what we aim to be for the families we serve: a warm, dependable presence that brightens every day.

Suraj Home Care Ltd. is an Edmonton-based home care provider offering a full range of in-home support services across Alberta. We help seniors, adults living with disabilities or chronic conditions, and people recovering from illness or surgery stay safe and independent at home.

Our team combines professional training with genuine compassion. We take time to understand each person's story, preferences and goals, and we build care around them — never the other way around.

Our Caregivers

Carefully selected. Professionally trained.

  • Police information & vulnerable sector checks
  • Health Care Aide (HCA) or relevant care experience

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Edmonton and provide home care in Edmonton, surrounding communities and across Alberta. Contact us to confirm availability in your area.

How quickly can care begin?

In many cases we can begin care within a few days of your free consultation — and sooner for urgent hospital-to-home situations, depending on caregiver availability.

Is there a minimum number of hours?

Our scheduling is flexible. We offer short visits, daily care, overnight shifts and 24-hour care. We'll recommend a schedule that fits your needs and budget during the consultation.

Are your caregivers screened?

Yes. All caregivers complete police information and vulnerable sector checks, reference checks, interviews, and hold relevant training and First Aid/CPR certification.

Can we change the care plan later?

Absolutely. Care needs change over time, and we review and adjust care plans regularly with you and your family.

How do I get started?

Call us at 780-813-2777 or fill out the form on our Contact page. We'll arrange a free, no-obligation in-home consultation.
